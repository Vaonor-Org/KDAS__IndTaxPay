Our Story

A legacy of precision,
a future of innovation

K.Das & Associates was founded with a singular mission — to bring world-class chartered accountancy services to every business and individual across India. What began as a boutique CA practice in Kolkata has grown into a trusted multi-service compliance firm, now amplified by the IndTaxPay digital platform.

We believe compliance should never be a burden. Our expert CAs combine deep regulatory knowledge with a human-first approach — guiding you through ITR filing, GST, payroll, audits and company registration with clarity and confidence.
