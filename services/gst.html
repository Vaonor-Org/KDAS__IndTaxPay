Why File on Time?

The cost of missing deadlines

💸
Avoid Late Fees
GSTR late fees can reach ₹200/day per return. ITR late filing attracts ₹5,000 penalty and interest under 234A/B/C.
🔄
Input Tax Credit
On-time GSTR-1 filing allows your buyers to claim input tax credit — critical for B2B relationships.
💰
Maximum Refunds
CA-reviewed returns identify all eligible deductions and exemptions — maximizing your refund or minimising tax.
🏦
Loan Eligibility
Filed ITRs are mandatory for home loans, business loans, and credit card applications at most banks.
🛡️
Avoid Scrutiny
Consistent, accurate filing history reduces the probability of income tax scrutiny notices and assessments.
🌍
Visa Applications
Most countries require 3 years of filed ITRs for visa applications — stay visa-ready with consistent compliance.
