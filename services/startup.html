Why Register?

What a registered company
gives you

🛡️
Limited Liability
Your personal assets are protected from business debts and legal obligations.
💰
Raise Funding
Pvt Ltd companies can issue shares and accept VC, angel, and bank funding.
✅
Brand Credibility
Clients, vendors, and partners trust a formally registered legal entity.
🏦
Business Banking
Open a current account, accept GST invoices, and build business credit history.
🎯
MSME & Govt Schemes
Access government tenders, subsidies, and priority lending after registration.

Perpetual Existence
The company exists independently of its founders — even if you exit or change.
