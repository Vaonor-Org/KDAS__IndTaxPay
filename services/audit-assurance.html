The Process

How we conduct
your audit

Phase 1
Audit Planning
Scope definition, materiality determination, risk assessment, and audit programme preparation by the lead CA.
Phase 2
Data Collection
Collection of books of accounts, vouchers, bank statements, contracts, and supporting documents from the client team.
Phase 3
Field Work & Testing
Substantive testing of transactions, analytical procedures, internal control evaluation, and compliance verification.
Phase 4
Management Discussion
Discussion of audit findings, clarification of queries, and review of management representations before report finalisation.
Phase 5
Report & Certification
CA-signed audit report, management letter, and all required statutory annexures delivered and filed with relevant authorities.
